My research interests lie in numerical analysis, approximation theory, splines, fractals, their interplay, and practical applications. I was a researcher in mathLab at the International School for Advanced Studies (SISSA). Prior to that, I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science at the University of Florence. I earned my Ph.D. at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi under the mentorship of Prof. P. Viswanathan.
